Employees Federal Credit Union (EFCU) Study

Author

Ruben Garcia

1 Introduction & Research Overview

Employees Federal Credit Union (EFCU) is experiencing a lower loan/share ratio than comparable credit unions, leading to accumulated surplus funds and squeezed profit margins. This research project investigates why members borrow money from alternative financial institutions rather than EFCU and evaluates member attitudes toward credit union management, service proficiencies, and regional differences.

3 Analytical Rationale & Procedure

3.1 Descriptive & Frequency Analysis

For binary and multiple-choice questions (e.g., Q2, Q28, Q29, and service awareness Q13–Q26), frequency tables and percentages will be generated to establish baseline member characteristics and identify predominant borrowing choices.

3.2 Hypothesis Testing

  1. One-Sample \(t\)-test (H1): Evaluates whether mean ratings for employee courteousness, helpfulness, professionalism, and availability significantly exceed the neutral midpoint of 3.0 on a 5-point Likert scale.
  2. Independent Samples \(t\)-test / Chi-Square (H2): Compares responses between HQ residents (\(Q38 = 1\)) and non-HQ residents (\(Q38 = 2\)). Continuous items (Q30, Q32, Q37) are evaluated with \(t\)-tests, while categorical items (Q39) use Chi-Square (\(\chi^2\)) test of independence.
  3. Correlation Analysis (H4): Assesses the directional relationship between rate perceptions (Q8, Q30) and overall credit union management satisfaction (Q37).
